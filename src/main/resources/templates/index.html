Bitplane

Encode files into videos and decode videos back into files.

Bitplane explores the concept behind the "YouTube Infinite Storage Glitch": converting arbitrary files into video data that can later be reconstructed back into their original form with perfect accuracy.

Lossless Encoding • Frame Based Protocol • Open Source
